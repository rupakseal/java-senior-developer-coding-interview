# Evaluation Areas

The interview uses evidence from your code, tests, and explanation. The interviewer considers:

1. **Problem discovery and prioritization** — Do you identify the highest-risk defects and choose a realistic scope?
2. **Java implementation quality** — Is the code readable, cohesive, and correct?
3. **Resilience and concurrency** — Are timeouts, retries, state transitions, and concurrent callers handled safely?
4. **Security and privacy** — Are tokens and customer information protected, and does validation fail safely?
5. **Testing** — Are tests deterministic and focused on meaningful behavior and failure modes?
6. **Observability** — Can operators distinguish success, rejection, timeout, open circuit, and dependency failure?
7. **Communication and ownership** — Can you explain your decisions, limitations, and production rollout?
8. **AI-assisted engineering, when used** — Do you provide useful constraints, review suggestions critically, and verify the result?

You are not expected to implement every possible improvement. Depth, correctness, and evidence matter more than the number of changes.
